# Failure log

Where the pipeline broke the car (or the shot), how it showed up, and what changed.

| # | Failure | How we found out | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Image-edit models redraw the whole frame: wheel spokes, badges and lamp shapes drift even when the car is in the input image. | Edit runs with the car in the guide; spoke counts and badge shapes compared against the GLB render. | The image model never sees a car to keep: it paints an empty plate, the Cycles car render is composited on top. `car_pixels_untouched` asserts the opaque car pixels of every composite equal the render (≤ 2 levels, ≥ 99.5 %). |
| 2 | Grey stand-ins (hills, walls, buildings) showed up as reflections in the paint. | Grey blobs visible in the bodywork of early composites. | Stand-ins are visible to camera and shadow rays only, hidden from glossy and diffuse rays. |
| 3 | Seams around painted edges after depth-of-field blur. | Halo where a painted mountain edge did not line up with the stand-in edge behind it. | Blur by the plate's own depth (Depth Anything V2 Small, calibrated to metres on the road against Blender's exact road depth) instead of Blender's depth. |
| 4 | Every Alps shot came out as the same hairpin from the same angle. | Three runs, near-identical framing; traced to location examples ("hairpin", "switchbacks") in the planner prompt and schema docs. | Removed recipes and location examples; the planner designs road, set, camera and lens per location (`ShotPlan`). |
| 5 | Run crashed with `KeyError: 'camera'` in `plate_prompt` after an approval was answered twice (approve and revise from two tabs). | The second answer made ADK replay the invocation and skip `setup_scene`; seen in the run error and the session events. | `RunGuard` middleware returns 409 for a second concurrent run and for answers to approvals that are no longer pending; the UI reopens the session read-only. |
| 6 | Resuming the corrupted run failed again with the same error. | `[ resume ]` replayed the broken invocation in 5 s with no images. | Continue with a new chat message in the same session; memory and approved shots carry over. |
| 7 | Ladakh edit ("camera near ground, front-side, moving shot") burned all 8 images with no result. | The planner put the camera at road height looking 1.3° up. Blender's guide came out as grey ground, flat sky and one horizon line: no road edges, no mountains. The image model painted a good road and peaks anyway, but `layout_match` scored 0.03–0.14 (pass 0.25) on every plate, and the automatic retry sent the same useless guide back 4 times. | New `guide_readable` check approves the guide before any image is made (lines must span 15 % of the frame height, road must cover 3 % of the frame; the good guides measure 41–100 % and 15–49 %, this one 1 % and 0.2 %). A rejected guide goes to the repair LLM, which changes the camera or set. A layout mismatch that repeats on the next attempt also goes to repair instead of another identical retry. |
| 8 | Shots kept looking down on the car. | The user noticed it; the approved shots had the camera 3.4 m (Alps, 10°), 7.9 m (Ladakh, 20°) and 2.3 m (coast, 5°) above the road. The plan schema called 10-25° "eye level", but at the 15-20 m the planner works at, 10° is a ladder and 20° a crane. | The schema and the planner prompt now give the camera height per angle (2-3° = eye level, 10° = ladder, 20° = crane, 35°+ = drone). The next Alps plan chose 2.5°. |
| 9 | The new guide gate rejected every guide ("road covers 0.0 %"), and the repair loop used up its turns. | A scratch run after adding the gate: six rejected guides in a row, all with road 0.0 %, while the same road mask measured 34 % offline. The node passes the mask already as true/false, and the check thresholded it again. | The check accepts a mask that is already true/false; the unit test now feeds it the way the node does. Re-run: guide approved, the review card appeared, revise rebuilt the guide. No images were spent in either run. |
| 10 | Wheels broke in motion: rims, spokes, brake discs and hubs are separate meshes in the GLB, and only the tyre turned with `speed_kmh`. | Lab renders of the moving car (`scripts/dev/lab.py`). | `load_car` finds every mesh centred on a wheel's axle and spins it with that wheel; the caliper sits off the axle and stays with the body (`ts_load_car_toolcode.py`). |
| 11 | The car itself smeared in rolling shots: with a long shutter and imperfect tracking, badges and spokes blurred. | Rolling-shot tests in the lab; `tests/test_scene.py` covers 1 m of travel. | The car's own travel during the exposure is capped at 2 cm (`MAX_BODY_SLIP_M`, `camera.py`) whatever the shutter; only the plate streaks. |
| 12 | The tool log stored a text copy of each generated plate's PNG bytes, while the brief says the database keeps keys, never image bytes. | Reading `ts_tool_log` rows while totting up spend. | Logged results are made JSON-safe first: bytes become `<N bytes>`, arrays `<array shape dtype>`; images stay in S3 only. |
